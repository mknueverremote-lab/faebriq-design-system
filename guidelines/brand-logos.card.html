Guidelines / Identity

Brand logos

The FÆBRIQ mark exists in three sanctioned lockups — wordmark, stacked, and Æ monogram — each set in Instrument Serif on the near-black ground #0f0e0c. Every lockup carries the pride circuit trace, a thin five-node gradient line (red → yellow → green → blue → purple) that threads through the mark as the brand's unifying signal.

Clear space

Reserve a minimum margin equal to the cap-height of the Æ on every side of any lockup. Nothing — type, imagery, or the circuit trace of another mark — may enter that zone.

Minimum size

Wordmark and stacked lockups should not render below 120px wide; the Æ monogram is the only lockup approved for favicons and sub-32px placements.

Ground & contrast

Default to off-white #f5f5f5 type on the near-black ground #0f0e0c. On light or photographic grounds, flip to slate #2f3e46 and keep the circuit trace at 60–80% opacity so it never competes with the wordmark.

Do

  • Let the circuit trace run through the wordmark as a single unbroken line — it is the mark's signature, not a decoration applied after the fact.
  • Pair the wordmark lockup with "Code it, serve it." and the stacked lockup with the shorter "Serve it." cue.
  • Use the Æ monogram alone wherever space is tight: favicons, stickers, corner marks on accessories.

Don't

  • Recolor the circuit trace to a single brand color, stretch it, or break it into segments — the five-node gradient is fixed.
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or gradients to the Instrument Serif letterforms; let the high-contrast ground do the work.
  • Crowd the lockup with secondary lockups, taglines, or imagery inside its clear-space margin.
